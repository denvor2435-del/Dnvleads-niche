## 🇬🇧 UK notes (my additions, not from the course)
> **My notes:** The course is U.S.-based. Everything here is my own adaptation and must be checked with real UK owners or sources.
- Course job values (3.5) are U.S. figures. Get real UK average job values from owners.
- Cold calling: B2B calls are legal under PECR. Screen numbers against TPS/CTPS (many sole traders are registered).
- A2P 10DLC (Module 8) is U.S.-only. UK SMS has no equivalent registration, but check GHL's UK sender ID rules.
- Re-run the break-even formula in £ before copying U.S. retainer figures (3.15, 3.18).
- Shortlist candidates: loft conversions, garden rooms, driveways (also: extensions, orangeries, kitchens, heat pumps, landscaping renovations).
